# Discovery Insights Report

Generated from AI-powered review analysis of **577** normalized quick-commerce user signals (649 raw). Sources: App Store, Play Store, Reddit, forums, social, product reviews — scraped via `tools/discovery-pipeline` + manual input via `apps/collect`.

**Last updated:** 2026-07-30 · **Validation:** 10/10 themes passed · **Demo guide:** [DISCOVERY_DEMONSTRATION.md](./DISCOVERY_DEMONSTRATION.md)

## Executive summary

Users repeat the same 1–2 categories because **reorder loops**, **trust anxiety**, and **broken in-app discovery** combine to block exploration. Social proof and trial pricing are the strongest levers for first purchase in a new category.

## Top themes (validated)

### 1. Reorder loops create category lock-in
- **Research Q:** Why do users repeatedly buy from the same categories?
- **Frequency:** 56 | **Confidence:** High
- **Insight:** "Buy Again" delivers in one tap — users never see adjacent categories.
- **Action:** Post-order nudge for one adjacent category (not at checkout).

### 2. 10-minute promise optimizes for speed, not exploration
- **Research Q:** What role do habits play?
- **Frequency:** 86 | **Confidence:** High
- **Action:** Async explore moment post-delivery, separate from urgent orders.

### 3. Unfamiliar categories feel risky
- **Research Q:** What prevents exploration?
- **Frequency:** 79 | **Confidence:** High
- **Action:** Trial packs, bestseller badges, easy returns.

### 4. In-app discovery fails for unknown unknowns
- **Research Q:** How do users discover products today?
- **Frequency:** 93 | **Confidence:** High
- **Action:** "People like you tried" recommendations from order history.

### 5. Social proof drives first trial
- **Research Q:** How do users discover products today?
- **Frequency:** 91 | **Confidence:** High
- **Action:** Anonymized social proof in nudges.

### 6. Too many options paralyze quick decisions
- **Research Q:** What information before trying new category?
- **Frequency:** 22 | **Confidence:** High
- **Action:** Curated starter packs (3–5 SKUs).

### 7. Promos and trial pricing unlock experimentation
- **Research Q:** What information before trying new category?
- **Frequency:** 60 | **Confidence:** High
- **Action:** ₹99 trial SKUs with free-delivery thresholds.

### 8. Students experiment more; essentials buyers cautious
- **Research Q:** Which segments experiment?
- **Frequency:** 41 | **Confidence:** High
- **Action:** Segment-specific nudge strategies.

### 9. Life-stage transitions are missed
- **Research Q:** What unmet needs emerge?
- **Frequency:** 18 | **Confidence:** Medium
- **Action:** Life-event triggers (new parent, pet adoption).

### 10. One bad first purchase stops exploration
- **Research Q:** What frustrations emerge repeatedly?
- **Frequency:** 68 | **Confidence:** High
- **Action:** Highest-rated SKUs only for first category orders.

## Hypotheses for Phase 2

1. Weekly essentials buyers avoid exploration due to reorder loops, not lack of interest.
2. Trust anxiety blocks personal care more than price.
3. Social proof + trial packs drive first category trial.
4. In-app discovery fails — post-order async nudges beat homepage banners.
5. Students experiment via social; essentials buyers need starter packs.

## Target segment

**Weekly essentials repeat buyers** — 3+ orders/month, groceries/household focus, ≤2 categories. Highest volume with clearest nudge opportunity.
