# User Interview Guide

**Segment:** Weekly essentials repeat buyers  
**Duration:** 30 minutes  
**Goal:** Validate or challenge Phase 1 AI discovery themes

---

## Intro (3 min)

- Thank participant; explain purpose (improving category discovery, not selling)
- Request permission to record for notes only
- Emphasize no right/wrong answers

---

## Warm-up: Shopping routine (5 min)

1. Walk me through your last quick-commerce order. What triggered it?
2. Which app(s) do you use most? Why that one?
3. How many times per month do you typically order?

**Probe for:** habit, speed, reorder behavior (Theme: reorder loops, speed-transactional)

---

## Category behavior (8 min)

4. What categories do you buy most often? Has this changed over time?
5. Tell me about a time you tried a **new category** on quick commerce. What happened?
6. Is there a category you'd like to buy on quick commerce but haven't? What stopped you?

**Probe for:** trust/risk, bad first experience, choice overload (Themes: trust-risk, bad-first-experience, choice-overload)

---

## Discovery and exploration (7 min)

7. How do you usually find products on the app — search, homepage, recommendations, or something else?
8. Have friends or social media ever influenced you to try something new on quick commerce?
9. What would make you comfortable trying personal care / pet / baby products on Blinkit?

**Probe for:** discovery friction, social proof, incentives (Themes: discovery-friction, social-wom, incentives)

---

## Concept reaction (5 min)

_Show mock nudge card: "You buy groceries weekly — 847 users also tried personal care starter pack ₹99"_

10. How would you react to this after placing an order?
11. Would you prefer this at checkout, after delivery, or via notification?
12. What would make you dismiss it immediately?

---

## Wrap-up (2 min)

13. Anything else about shopping on quick commerce we didn't cover?
14. Thank and explain next steps

---

## Phase 1 hypotheses to validate

| # | Hypothesis | Related theme IDs |
|---|------------|-------------------|
| H1 | Reorder loops block exploration, not lack of interest | theme-habit-reorder |
| H2 | Trust anxiety blocks personal care more than price | theme-trust-risk |
| H3 | Social proof + trial packs drive first category trial | theme-social-wom, theme-incentives |
| H4 | Homepage discovery fails; post-order nudges preferred | theme-discovery-friction |
| H5 | Essentials buyers need starter packs, not full browse | theme-choice-overload |
