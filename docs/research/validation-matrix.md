## Summary

- **Primary barrier confirmed:** Trust anxiety + reorder habit loops (H1, H2)
- **Strongest lever confirmed:** Trial packs + social proof post-order (H3)
- **Challenged:** Not all users want fewer choices — offer curated AND full browse
- **New insight (not in AI discovery):** Post-delivery moment preferred over checkout nudge (5/6) — checkout feels rushed
