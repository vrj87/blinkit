# Interview Screener — Weekly Essentials Repeat Buyers

## Target segment

Users who order groceries and household essentials on quick-commerce apps **3+ times per month** but have purchased from **≤2 categories** in the last 90 days.

## Screening questions

1. How often do you use quick-commerce apps (Blinkit, Instamart, BigBasket)?  
   - **Qualify:** 3+ times per month

2. What categories do you typically buy?  
   - **Qualify:** Primarily groceries and/or household essentials

3. Have you ever purchased personal care, pet supplies, baby products, or frozen foods on quick commerce?  
   - **Qualify:** No, or only once and didn't repeat

4. When you open the app, do you usually reorder from history or browse new categories?  
   - **Qualify:** Reorder from history / same list

5. Age and city (for segment tagging)

## Disqualifiers

- Uses quick commerce less than once per month
- Already buys from 3+ categories regularly
- Works at a quick-commerce company
