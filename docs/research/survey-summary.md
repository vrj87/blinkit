**Questionnaire:** [Blinkit Shopping Behaviour Survey](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScAJAfUjeyQm-bw0qCQdlk2LKf0RM0S5bhXcIFsdiLjgRHSAg/viewform?pli=1)

Academic research survey (~3 minutes) with active quick-commerce shoppers. Used as **primary research** in deck slide 3; supplemented by 6 semi-structured interviews for depth.

## Sample profile (n = 40)

| Dimension | Finding |
|-----------|---------|
| **Age** | 100% aged 25–34 |
| **Occupation** | 100% working professionals |
| **Order frequency** | 2–3×/week (majority) · weekly · monthly |
| **Apps used** | Blinkit · Instamart · BigBasket · Amazon/Flipkart |
| **Typical basket** | Groceries · snacks · beverages · household · personal care |
| **Repeat behaviour** | Often / sometimes / always buy same products |

Aligns with deck slide 4: **25–34 dominant age group · 2–3 orders/week · habitual basket**.

## Key findings

### What triggers a new category (when users have tried)

| Trigger | Frequency |
|---------|-----------|
| Discount / sale | High |
| Needed urgently | High |
| Advertisement / in-app recommendation | Medium |
| Friend / recommendation | Medium |
| Curiosity while browsing | Low–medium |

### Why users don't explore new categories

| Barrier | Maps to deck |
|---------|--------------|
| Happy with current routine | Habit satisfaction |
| Don't know what's available | Low awareness |
| Too many choices | Choice overload |
| No trust in unfamiliar brands | Trust gap |
| Takes too much time | Speed / transactional habit |
| No recommendations | Discovery friction |

**Top 4 barriers in deck are confirmed** across open-ended and multi-select responses.

### Information needed before trying something new

1. **Reviews & ratings** — cited by ~95% of respondents  
2. **Price comparison** — ~80%  
3. **Product description** — ~75%  
4. **Best-seller tag** — ~60%  
5. Friend recommendation — ~25%

### What would motivate trial

1. **Discounts** — most selected  
2. **Personalized recommendations** — high  
3. **Bundle offers** — high  
4. **Better product explanation** — high  
5. Free samples · cashback — medium

Directly supports **Smart Category Explorer** pillars: explained recommendations, AI summaries, bundles & first-try offers.
