## Slide 4: Ordering is habitual; exploring a new category is not

- 25–34 dominant age; 2–3× orders/week; 4 apps in rotation
- What they buy: groceries, snacks, household care
- Triggers: urgent need, discounts, curiosity, friend recommendation

## Slide 5: Four barriers stop users from trying a new category

1. Low awareness  2. Trust gap  3. Choice overload  4. Habit satisfaction  
What users need: reviews/ratings · price comparison · clear info · personalised reason
